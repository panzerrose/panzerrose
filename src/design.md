# Panzerrose Design Language

## Goal

A calm, low-contrast military-inspired theme that minimizes eye strain while preserving clear visual hierarchy.

## Palette

- Deep Gunmetal
- Panzer Grey
- Raised Panzer Grey
- Dark Brown Black
- Warm Ivory
- Rose Gold
- Muted Olive
- Sand

## Rules

- Rose Gold is reserved for active/focused UI.
- Olive is used for secondary information.
- Sand highlights data and constants.
- Large surfaces differ by subtle value changes, not by saturation.
